# Idea: "saved views" for the incident dashboard

Rough notes from the Tuesday sync. Nothing decided.

---

People keep re-applying the same filters on the incident dashboard every
morning. Severity, service, on-call rotation, time window. Someone suggested we
let them save a filter combination and come back to it.

Open thoughts:

- Is a saved view private, or can you share it with your team?
- If you share it and then change it, does the other person's copy change too?
- What happens to a saved view when the service it filters on gets renamed or
  deleted?
- Do we want a "team default" view that new members land on?
- Nobody has asked for this. It came out of a support conversation where an SRE
  said they had "the same six clicks every morning".

That's it. No PRD, no designs, no requirements. We want to know whether this is
a real thing and what it would actually have to do before we scope it.
