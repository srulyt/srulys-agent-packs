# Concept note: Pinned Insights

Analysts in our reporting product rebuild the same handful of views every
Monday. In three customer calls last month, people described keeping a
personal doc of "the five charts I actually look at" and pasting links into
it.

Hypothesis: if users could star a chart or metric and see all their starred
items on one page ("Pinned Insights"), they would open the product more often
at the start of the week and spend less time navigating.

Things I don't know yet:

- Whether this is a personal page only or something a team can share.
- Whether starred items should refresh live or show a snapshot.
- How many users would bother to star anything.

I might propose this next quarter. For now I just want to get it on paper.
