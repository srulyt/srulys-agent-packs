# Evidence Provenance

Assign immutable `claim_id`, `evidence_id`, and `source_id`. Preserve locator, publisher, dates, excerpt/summary, confidence, verification status, allowed claims/use, and qualification. Every factual slide assertion maps to ledger evidence in footer and notes. Unsupported claims remain explicitly unsupported, qualified, excluded, or raised to the user. Public research permission never permits disclosure of supplied/private context in queries.

## Boundaries
This skill provides domain knowledge only. It grants no workflow, state, publication, review-verdict, web-disclosure, or file-write authority. Follow the invoking agent's boundaries and the v3 schemas.
