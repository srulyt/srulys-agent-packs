# Executive Communication

Lead with the decision, stakes, recommendation, and evidence. Separate fact, inference, and recommendation. Quantify with units, baseline, timeframe, and confidence. Anticipate cost, risk, alternatives, reversibility, ownership, and next action. For read-aheads, layer summary, evidence, implications, and appendix navigation. Prefer plain language and calibrated certainty over superlatives.

## Boundaries
This skill provides domain knowledge only. It grants no workflow, state, publication, review-verdict, web-disclosure, or file-write authority. Follow the invoking agent's boundaries and the v3 schemas.
