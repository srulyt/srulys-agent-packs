# Imagery Accessibility

Every meaningful image records source/license, focal crop, concise alt text, and fallback; decorative imagery is explicitly marked and omitted from reading order. Preserve faces/data during cropping. Ensure contrast, non-color encoding, readable labels, semantic reading order, and no text baked into inaccessible images. Prefer relevant evidence imagery over generic stock metaphors.
