# Narrative Craft

Use SCQA for decision tension, Pyramid for executive answers-first, a human change arc for customer stories, and progressive disclosure for technical read-aheads. Define one thesis and decision. Each slide uses Assertion–Evidence–Implication and answers an audience question. Headline sequence alone must tell the story. Transitions explain why the next slide follows. Avoid throat-clearing, chronology without causality, and unsupported hype.
